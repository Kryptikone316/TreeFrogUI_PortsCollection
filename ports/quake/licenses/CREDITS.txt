Quake (Datafrog SF3500 port)

Engine: TyrQuake by Kevin Shanahan, based on id Software's Quake source release,
  with the libretro core by the libretro team and contributors,
  https://github.com/libretro/tyrquake
  GNU General Public License, version 2 or later (../LICENSE-TYRQUAKE).
SF3500 changes (build flags, string-reference repair, joypad binds, real-time audio,
  hunk sizing, logging, the game loader): AthruZ,
  https://github.com/Kryptikone316/tyrquake (branch sf3500). Distributed under the
  same GPL terms as the rest of the program.
Game: Quake, id Software, 1996. No game data is included: pak0.pak and pak1.pak,
  the mission packs and the music are the player's own. Quake is a trademark of
  id Software; the box art shows its logo as a floppy-disk icon (fan art by the
  port author) only to identify the game.
Third-party code compiled into the core: libFLAC, libogg, libvorbis and Tremor
  (BSD-style licences, Xiph.Org Foundation) and libretro-common (MIT). Their
  licence texts are in THIRD-PARTY.txt.
Game loader (quakeloader.mipsel): written for this port. Its display wrapper and
  gamepad reader are the small modules shared by the SF3500 ports, derived from
  TreeFrogUI's pico286 SF3000 patch; its fallback font is font8x8 by Daniel
  Hepper, public domain (font8x8-license.txt).

Source code for this port is the pinned "source" submodule of this repository.
