Bitmap font (source/noncairo/font2.c)
==============================================================================

The file's own header reads: "Apple IIgs emulator - Apple 2GS Original fonts. All the characters
are coded in their original set. Only the USA keyboard is recognized with ROM 01." It gives no
licence or author. It is shipped as is in libretro/libretro-2048, and is included here only
because the core's compiled text uses it.
