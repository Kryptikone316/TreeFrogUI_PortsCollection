Shareware DOOM (DOOM1.WAD, the first episode) is included and ready to play.

To play the full games, copy your own game files into this folder: DOOM.WAD
(Ultimate Doom), DOOM2.WAD, PLUTONIA.WAD or TNT.WAD. Add-on level files (.wad)
that were made for Doom or Doom II can go here too. When there is more than one,
a small loader screen lets you choose what to start. See the setup guide
(docs/setup/chocolatedoom.md in the Ports Collection) for where to find the files.

Only the shareware episode is included; the commercial game files are not.
