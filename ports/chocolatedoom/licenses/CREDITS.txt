Chocolate Doom (Datafrog SF3500 port)

Engine: Chocolate Doom 3.1.1, by Simon Howard and contributors,
  https://github.com/chocolate-doom/chocolate-doom
  GNU General Public License, version 2 or later (chocolate-doom-COPYING.md).
Game: Doom, id Software, 1993. gamedata/DOOM1.WAD is the shareware episode,
  unmodified (sha256 1d7d43be501e67d927e415e0b8f3e29c3bf33075e859721816f652a526cac771).
  Basis for including it: id's shareware release may be copied unmodified at no
  charge, and John Carmack's email of 23 October 1999 says "The DOOM shareware
  wad is freely distributable"; Debian (doom-wad-shareware) and RPM Fusion
  distribute it on the same basis. The commercial game files (DOOM.WAD,
  DOOM2.WAD, PLUTONIA.WAD, TNT.WAD) are NOT included; players bring their own.
SF3500 platform backend, music changes and the WAD loader: AthruZ,
  https://github.com/Kryptikone316/chocolate-doom (branch sf3500). Distributed
  under the same GPL terms as the rest of the program.
Device selection through /tmp/tfdevice.env: from TreeFrogUI's hijack script
  (zhijack.tpl.sh), pointed out by Maheshivara. The display wrapper is derived
  from TreeFrogUI's pico286 SF3000 patch.
WAD loader: its normal font, logo and cursor are read at run time from the
  player's own game file and are not copied. The built-in fallback font is
  font8x8 by Daniel Hepper, public domain (font8x8-license.txt).
No music pack is included: the game plays the music stored in the game file.
SDL2 is provided by the device's firmware and linked dynamically; it is not
included here.

Source code for this port is the pinned "source" submodule of this repository.
